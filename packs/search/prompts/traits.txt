POND COVERAGE

establish distributed systems, product engineering or growth engineering experience. A pond explicitly
scoring distributed systems already covers that experience and its ordinary parts, even if worded
differently. Return the useful qualifications beyond that coverage.

GROUPING

compatibility, testing, packaging and releases. Prefer one such category to its task or tool inventory.
Compare the proposed qualifiers with each other: merge broad and narrow descriptions of the same
experience. Keep separate qualifiers when they add meaningfully different backgrounds, such as
full-stack development and distributed systems design. A requirement already represented by a
retained qualifier needs no additional trait.
Do not create redundant technology or language traits. Keep each technology or language requirement
in the single qualifier that best represents it; remove overlapping mentions from other trait labels.

Keep explicit background qualifications such as an MD, PhD or clearance separately when relevant.
Preserve accepted alternatives. Routine tools and languages, years, seniority, generic ownership,
soft skills, work authorization, location and compensation are not additional experience qualifiers.
