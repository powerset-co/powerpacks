state.dossier holds two contact records, A and B: each contact's name, my relationship to them, key identity facts, what we talk about, and sample messages (how I talk to them and how they talk to me). Decide whether A and B are the SAME PERSON. Reason HOLISTICALLY over ALL the evidence; no single signal dominates.

Weigh these together with careful reasoning:
- IDENTITY: name including nicknames/short forms (e.g. Annmay vs Ann), middle initials, employer, school, location, emails/handles, and any hard CONTRADICTIONS.
- ROLE IN MY LIFE: continuity of a specific personal relationship can support identity. Being contacts at the same vendor, employer, or account does not: multiple coworkers can play the same professional role.
- CONTENT & BEHAVIOR: what we actually do — e.g. forwarding household receipts, reservations, or logistics to me is intimate/family behavior; coordinating deals is professional. Behavior supports identity only when it connects the same individual; coworkers can discuss the same deal in the same way.
- TONE/REGISTER, only WHEN available: consistent register supports same person; a clear formal-vs-intimate mismatch can indicate different people. If one record has NO messages from me, you simply cannot use tone — treat its absence as neutral, never as evidence.
- SHARED IDENTIFIERS: the normalized values really match, but attribution from contact records or message extraction can be wrong. A shared number may belong to the mailbox owner, an office, a household, or a third party. Check who actually owns it using the messages and identity facts. If the supplied evidence does not attribute it to one person, treat overlap as neutral.
- DISTINCT PEOPLE: different full names, separate work emails, and different roles at the same organization usually describe coworkers. Shared company, deal, conversational topics, or an office phone do not make coworkers the same person. Merge only with evidence connecting the identities, such as a recognizable nickname (Bob/Robert), changed name, or clearly attributable personal identifier. Do not assume that missing contradictory details establish a match.

A shared or similar NAME ALONE is not enough — but a similar name PLUS aligned role/identity/behavior is strong. Set same_person=true only when the COMBINED evidence supports it; otherwise false.
