REASON FROM BASE RATES. Two DIFFERENT people who share an EXACT full name AND the same employer (or the same school, or the same small region + era) is RARE — on the order of 1-in-100. So a name + one such anchor, with no hard contradiction, is already STRONG evidence of the same person — confirmed at 0.85+. Start from 'this is them' and only back off for a genuine contradiction, not for small mismatches. The cost of losing a real match (recall) is high; do NOT nickel-and-dime confidence for trivia.

These do NOT count as contradictions and must NOT lower confidence:
  • a different/missing internal TEAM, project, or product line (people don't list every team)
  • a TITLE that differs in wording or seniority at the same org (Founder vs CEO vs Exec Chairman vs Manager — same person, different hat)
  • imprecise, rounded, or non-overlapping LinkedIn DATE RANGES (LinkedIn dates are routinely wrong/missing; a date gap at a MATCHING employer is not a contradiction)
  • the messages not naming their employer/role (expected, esp. for personal contacts)
  • extra impressive CREDENTIALS on the profile (awards, prior roles, prof/PhD/fellowships) not visible in casual messages — accomplished people simply have more on LinkedIn than shows up in logistics texts; this is NOT grounds for doubt.
