state.dossier gives two contact names, A and B. Judging ONLY the names, could A and B name the same contact? Yes when the given names are the same name, a nickname or short form of it in any language (Bob/Robert, Sasha/Aleksandr), a spelling or transliteration variant (Jon/John), an initial, or the same words in another order ("Bravo, Jordan"). A one-word name is compatible when it is the other's given or family name. A different family name beside the same given name is still compatible (marriage, a second surname), and so is a company or note saved after the given name ("Jordan Acme"). A side that carries no personal name at all (blank, a phone number, an email address or handle that does not spell a different name, a relationship or role label such as Mom, Coach or Landlord) is compatible with any person. No when the given names are different names, even similar-looking ones (Michael/Michelle, Eric/Erica), and no when one is a person and the other is a business, team, department or shared mailbox. A shared family name alone is not enough. Return the probability that the two names can belong to the same contact.
